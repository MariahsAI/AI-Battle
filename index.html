Summary of the official rules

Rules & payouts

Eligibility

Open to creators 18 and older where the contest is legal. Employees of sponsors and the label partner can't enter.

Free entry

No purchase or payment is needed to enter or win. One entry per artist per season.

AI disclosure

Every entry lists the AI tools used. Human creative direction is required.

Rights & voices

You must own or license all material. Cloning a real artist's voice or likeness without written consent means disqualification.

Fan voting

Verified accounts only. 25 votes per account per day. Bots, vote buying and multiple accounts void the votes and can disqualify the entry.

Judging

From the Top 16 on, overall score is 60% fan rating and 40% judges panel (craft, originality, hit potential).

Prize payouts

Paid within 30 days of the finals after identity and tax-form verification (W-9 or W-8BEN). Winners are responsible for taxes.

Label showcase

The Final 3 get a showcase and A&R meeting. Any record deal is negotiated separately and is not guaranteed.

Your rights

You keep ownership of your music. You grant AI Battles a license to stream and promote your entry during and after the season.

Disputes

Decisions of the judges and organizer are final. Full terms are in the official rules document.

Questions

FAQ

Does my track have to be 100% AI?

No. Any amount of AI assistance qualifies, from AI beats under your vocals to fully generated tracks you directed. You just have to disclose the tools.

Can I enter a song that's already released?

Yes, as long as you control the rights and it isn't signed to a label that would block a new deal.

How do I get paid if I win?

Set your payout method in your creator dashboard. Before payment we verify your identity and collect a tax form, then pay within 30 days.

Why can't I vote more?

Each fan account gets 25 votes per day so no single person can swing a battle. Guests get 3 votes before signing up.

Who are the judges?

A panel of A&R reps and producers chosen with the label partner, announced before the Top 16.
